कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥
"Your authority lies only in the action itself, never in its fruits. Do not let the fruits of action be your motive, nor let yourself become attached to inaction."
Layered Transmission (Anvaya)
कर्मणिkarmaṇi — in prescribed duty (action)
एवeva — only / certainly
अधिकारःadhikāraḥ — right / authority
तेte — your
माmā — never
फलेषुphaleṣu — in the fruits / results
कदाचनkadācana — at any time
The Guru's Mirror

When you apply this to Yuddham, how do you fight fiercely for justice while maintaining zero attachment to the outcome?
