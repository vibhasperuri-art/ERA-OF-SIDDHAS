कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥
"Your authority lies only in the action itself, never in its fruits. Do not let the fruits of action be your motive, nor let yourself become attached to inaction."
