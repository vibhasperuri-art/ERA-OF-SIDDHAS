Era of Siddhas
Awakening
॥ अथातो ब्रह्मजिज्ञासा ॥

Now, the inquiry into Brahman begins.

Before the Gurukula can know you, it must meet you. These questions are not a test of knowledge — they are a mirror. Answer from the gut, not the head. There are no wrong answers. Only honest ones.

Question 1 of 4 — Your Starting Ground
Where do you find yourself right now on the path of Dharma?
Not where you wish you were — where you actually are.
A
Complete beginner — I know Dharma exists but I haven't seriously studied it
The seed has not yet touched soil
B
I've read some texts, attended discourses, have a basic foundation
The seed has sprouted but the roots are shallow
C
I've studied seriously — I know the Darshanas, have a Sadhana practice
The roots run deep but the branches seek more sky
D
Advanced — I've lived in or studied from a Gurukula or traditional lineage
The tree bears fruit. It now seeks to give shade.
Question 2 of 4 — The Fire Within
What is the deepest question that brought you here?
The Upanishads say: only the burning question finds the burning answer.
A
Who am I? What is the nature of consciousness and the Self?
The path of Jñāna — Tat Tvam Asi
B
How should I live? What is Dharma in the modern world?
The path of Karma — righteous action
C
Is God real? What is the nature of the Divine — Ishvara, Brahman, Shakti?
The path of Bhakti and Agama
D
How does ancient wisdom connect to modern science and reality?
The bridge between Ganita and Darshana
Question 3 of 4 — The Vow
The Gurukula is not a course you consume. It is a discipline you enter. What can you honestly commit to?
The Guru values honesty above ambition.
A
15–20 minutes of focused study daily, reflection journal weekly
Steady. The tortoise pace.
B
1 hour daily, deep dives on weekends, full marathon challenges
Serious intent. The warrior's pace.
C
Flexible — intense bursts when inspiration strikes, rest when life calls
The seeker trusts the tide.
Question 4 of 4 — The Guru Listens
In your own words: what do you bring to the Gurukula, and what do you hope to leave with?
No right answer. The Guru reads your sincerity, not your eloquence.
