Bhagavad Gita 2.38
सुखदुःखे समे कृत्वा लाभालाभौ जयाजयौ ।
ततो युद्धाय युज्यस्व नैवं पापमवाप्स्यसि ॥
sukha-duḥkhe same kṛtvā labhālābhau jayājayau |
tato yuddhāya yujyasva naivaṃ pāpam avāpsyasi ||
Treating alike happiness and distress, loss and gain, victory and defeat, engage in the battle for the sake of duty. Doing so, you will not incur sin.
