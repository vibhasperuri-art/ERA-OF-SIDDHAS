The Three Pillars of Siddha Vidya

Select your path. Every pillar is deeply supported by the foundational sciences of Yoga, Upasana (Ritual/Mantra), and Upanishads (Philosophy).

I

Nagara Nirmana

नगर निर्माण · City Building

Designing spaces where the divine is reality. Actions dedicated to Bhagavan and Loka Sangraha.

  • Yoga Cultivates spatial awareness and harmony between the body and the physical environment.
  • Upasana Sanctifies the land (Vastu) and aligns structures with celestial and energetic grids.
  • Upanishads Reveals how physical architecture reflects the architecture of the soul and the cosmos.
II

Rājya Palanam

राज्य पालनम् · Governance

The profound constituency and way of ruling to lead the world toward the ultimate goal of Rama Rājyam.

  • Yoga Ensures mental clarity and emotional stability when making empire-level decisions.
  • Upasana Aligns the leader's intellect (Buddhi) with divine will and cosmic order (Rta).
  • Upanishads Provides the ethical framework that power must serve the ultimate truth.
III

Yuddham

युद्धम् · The Martial Path

The science of conflict, Neeti, and protection of Dharma. Master the internal and external battlefield.

  • Yoga Grants physical resilience, nervous system control, and unshakeable focus in battle.
  • Upasana Invokes martial deities, building internal fire (Tejas) and unyielding resolve.
  • Upanishads Instills the philosophy of detached action and fearlessness in the face of mortality.
